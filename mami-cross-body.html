Sunshine Cross Body pouch

The Sunshine Cross Body Pouch, a pattern by Bagstock Designs. Faux leather with a butterfly quilting cotton, padded with sew-in foam.

Materials

  • Pattern: Sunshine Cross Body Pouch by Bagstock Designs
  • Fabrics: faux leather and quilting cotton
  • Stabilisers: sew-in foam and Pellon 101 interfacing
  • Hardware: 2 × ¾" D-rings, 2 × ¾" swivel hooks, 1 × ¾" slider, 1 yard of #5 zipper tape, 3 zipper pulls

How it went

I love how this one turned out. Getting it really neat was hard — three zippers and faux leather don't forgive much — but it was worth it.
