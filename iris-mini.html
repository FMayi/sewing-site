Iris mini Cross Body Bag

The Iris Mini Cross Body Bag, a pattern by Aquaint Stitch.

Materials

  • Pattern: Iris Mini Cross Body Bag by Aquaint Stitch
  • Fabrics: bonded poly nylon and faux leather
  • Stabiliser: Decovil Light
  • Hardware: 2 × ¾" D-rings, 1 × ¾" slider, 2 swivel hooks, 1 magnetic snap, 7" #5 zipper and pull

How it went

A simple project. The one thing I'd tell anyone making it: square up the base properly, or the bag won't stand straight.
