Krystal Convertible Bag

The Krystal Convertible Bag, a pattern by Linds Handmade.

Materials

  • Pattern: Krystal Convertible Bag by Linds Handmade
  • Exterior: bonded poly nylon
  • Lining: waterproof canvas
  • Stabiliser: Pellon 31F fusible base
  • Hardware: 3 × #5 zipper pulls, about 1 yard of #5 zipper tape, 2 × 1" D-rings, 45" of 1" nylon strapping

How it went

Fairly easy overall — I followed a video tutorial alongside the pattern instructions. The hardest part was attaching the round base without getting pleats. What worked: snipping the seam allowance, and using a stapler instead of clips or pins to hold it while I sewed.
